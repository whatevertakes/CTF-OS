## CTF-Relevant Patterns

- Confirm challenge scope and avoid unrelated private targets.
- Record clue text, names, handles, domains, image metadata, location hints, time windows, and language assumptions.
- Track sources with URL, access date, archive link, and screenshot when useful.
- Disambiguate identities and locations with multiple independent clues.

## CWE/CVE Mapping

- CVE/CWE usually does not apply to OSINT unless the challenge pivots into a technical artifact or public advisory.

## Canonical Papers And Deep Dives

- Archive workflows, geolocation methodology, and source-corroboration guidance are relevant; do not rely on a single social hit.

## When To Use

- Use for public-information CTF tasks involving names, handles, domains, images, archives, geolocation, or historical pages.

## When Not To Use

- Do not target private, sensitive, unrelated, or out-of-scope real-world individuals or systems.
