CTF-OS SANDBOX CAPABILITIES

Base: bash/curl/wget/nc/socat/jq, file/binutils/xxd/strings, gcc/g++/make,
Python, Git/ripgrep, pwntools, pycryptodome, sympy, z3, capstone,
unicorn, ROPgadget, and requests.

Category build targets add pwn, web, rev, crypto, or forensic packages. Nested
container engines and Codex are absent.

Crypto includes PARI/GP, gmpy2, fpylll/cysignals, z3, sympy, and pycryptodome. SageMath
is intentionally not an Ubuntu 24.04 package dependency; use an approved
separate image when a challenge specifically requires the full Sage runtime.

Challenge input is mounted read-only at /challenge. /work and /artifacts are
attempt-private, size-bounded tmpfs mounts. Files in /artifacts are exported
explicitly, on flag discovery, and before cleanup instead of after every command.
